Why firms take this seriously

  • In one real case, a firm paid about $18,000 extra, because one sub had no workers comp. Documented case, WorkCompConsultant.
  • In many states, work done on an expired license counts as work with no license at all, and the firm can lose the right to be paid for that work. State licensing laws.
  • If a sub's worker gets hurt and the sub has no insurance, the firm that hired them can end up paying the medical bills. Workers comp law in many states.
